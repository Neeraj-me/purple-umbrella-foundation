Flagship Annual Platform

A platform where young people don't just listen. They participate.

The Purple Umbrella Festival is the Foundation's flagship annual platform bringing together students, educators, experts, innovators, artists and communities to explore climate change through creativity, conversation and action. It is designed not as a conventional conference, but as an experience where young people are active participants rather than passive audiences.

Perform. Screen. Speak. Question. Debate. Build. Share.

The Festival Brings Together

Six tracks, one platform

Track 01

Theatre for Climate

Young performers use theatre to explore climate challenges and imagine pathways to action.

Track 02

Short Films & Documentary

A platform for young filmmakers to tell stories about climate, communities, nature and our changing world.

Track 03

Innovations

Students present ideas, prototypes and innovations addressing real-world challenges.

Track 04

Climate Talks

Young voices take the stage to share ideas, perspectives and solutions.

Track 05

Climate & Health & Mental Wellbeing Conclave

A space for students, experts and practitioners to explore the connection between climate change, health and mental wellbeing.

Track 06

Climate & Heritage Quiz

An engaging exploration of knowledge, culture, heritage, sustainability and the Sustainable Development Goals.

2026 Edition Theme

AI & Climate: Reimagining the Future Together

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way we work, learn, communicate and solve problems. At the same time, climate change demands new ways of thinking, collaborating and innovating. The Festival brings these two worlds together to ask: how can technology help us build a better future — and what responsibilities come with that power? Through student-led performances, films, innovations, talks, conversations and interactive experiences, Purple Umbrella 2026 creates a space to explore these questions together.

"AI & Climate" is the 2026 Festival theme only — not a permanent part of the Foundation's identity. Future editions will explore different themes each year.
