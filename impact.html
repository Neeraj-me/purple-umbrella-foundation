Our Impact

The scale of climate change demands the scale of participation.

Our long-term ambition is to empower one million young Climate Champions. But impact is not only about numbers.

Beyond the Numbers

Impact is also about moments

A student discovering that their voice matters.

A school turning climate education into action.

A young filmmaker telling a story that changes how people see an issue.

A student innovator finding a new way to solve an old problem.

A conversation between generations that creates a new perspective.

A citizen making a small change — and inspiring someone else to do the same.

We believe these moments add up.
