Purple Umbrella Foundation

Empowering One Million Young Climate Champions

We believe young people should not simply inherit the future. They should help create it. Purple Umbrella Foundation creates platforms that bring together climate education, creativity, innovation and citizen action — helping young people understand the world around them and become active participants in shaping its future.

Explore Our Work Become a Climate Champion

Climate change needs more than awareness.

It needs curiosity. It needs imagination. It needs ideas. It needs conversations. It needs innovation. And it needs people willing to act.

Purple Umbrella Foundation creates spaces where young people can learn about climate change, express their ideas, build solutions, engage with experts and turn awareness into action.

What We Do

Learn. Create. Innovate. Act.

Learn

Understand climate change through meaningful and experiential education.

Create

Tell climate stories through theatre, film, public speaking and creativity.

Innovate

Build ideas and solutions for real-world climate challenges.

Act

Turn individual choices and collective participation into measurable action.

2026 Edition Theme — Not a Permanent Identity

AI & Climate: Reimagining the Future Together

Artificial Intelligence is transforming how we learn, work, communicate and solve problems. Climate change is demanding new ways of thinking, collaborating and innovating. Purple Umbrella Festival 2026 brings these conversations together — exploring how AI may contribute to climate solutions, and the responsibilities that come with increasingly powerful technology.
