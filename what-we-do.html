What We Do

We create platforms for young people to learn, express, innovate and act

Our work spans four interconnected areas. None of them stand alone — together they move a young person from curiosity to meaningful action.

Learn

Climate Education

We help young people understand climate change beyond textbooks and headlines. We encourage curiosity, critical thinking and conversations around the environmental, social and human dimensions of a changing planet.

Create

Creativity & Expression

Climate conversations do not have to be limited to conferences and classrooms. Through theatre, short films, documentaries, storytelling and public speaking, we give young people a platform to communicate what climate change means to them.

See it at the Festival
Innovate

Innovation

Young people are not merely future problem-solvers. They are problem-solvers today. We create opportunities for students and young innovators to present ideas, technologies and solutions that can contribute to a more sustainable future.

Act

Citizen Action

Awareness matters. But action matters too. Through initiatives such as Climate Promise, we create simple and measurable ways for individuals, schools and communities to participate in climate action.
